NX-AudioControl is a homebrew project for Nintendo Switch designed to enhance the console's audio experience by providing additional audio-related settings.

Do you feel like your Switch doesn't sound loud enough? NX-AudioControl lets you push your console's audio further, so you can enjoy your favorite games with louder sound.

<br><br>

> Built as an overlay using **libtesla**, it is also compatible with **Ultrahand**.

---

## Features

- **MasterVolume:** Increase the console's maximum volume from 100% to 150%, 200%, 250%, or 300%.

- **Hearing safety system:** Prevents the MasterVolume from increasing on other audio outputs, provided they are not the console's own speakers.

---

## How to install?

Download the latest version from [releases](https://github.com/Fh1do/NX-AudioControl/releases), extract the file named NX-AudioControl.zip and put the atmosphere and switch folders in the root of your microSD card.

> [!IMPORTANT]
It's necessary to restart the console to load the patches correctly.
