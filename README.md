Khowel is a homebrew project for Nintendo Switch designed to enhance the console's audio experience by providing additional audio-related settings.

Do you feel like your Switch doesn't sound loud enough? Khowel lets you push your console's audio further, so you can enjoy your favorite games with louder sound.

## Features

- **MasterVolume:** Increase the console's maximum volume from 100% to 150%, 200%, 250%, or 300%.

<img width="427" height="240" alt="Unlocked mode" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9ca23d39-a266-431d-8cb2-735d248b494f" />
<br><br>

- **Hearing safety system:** Prevents the MasterVolume from increasing on other audio outputs, provided they are not the console's own speakers.

<img width="427" height="240" alt="Locked mode" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/236a0e3a-2e53-4c9d-bfc2-76c0d28a70fe" />

## How to install?

Download the latest version from [releases](https://github.com/Fh1do/NX-AudioControl/releases), extract the file named KFNS.zip and put the atmosphere and switch folders in the root of your microSD card.

> [!IMPORTANT]
It's necessary to restart the console to load the patches correctly.
